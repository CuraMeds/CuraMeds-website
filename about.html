About

Clinical intelligence, built to support the people delivering care.

CuraMeds is a clinical intelligence platform for healthcare professionals and hospitals — combining AI, trusted clinical guidelines, and hospital-specific configuration to support diagnosis, medication safety, coding, and documentation, end to end.

What CuraMeds is

CuraMeds helps clinicians think through a patient's presentation, checks medications for dangerous interactions, maps documented conditions to the right ICD-10 codes, and reviews clinical notes against NHIA documentation requirements — so hospitals are properly reimbursed for the care they provide.

Every suggestion CuraMeds makes is grounded in trusted guidelines and comes with a citation, so the clinician can verify it independently. CuraMeds does not diagnose patients and does not replace clinical judgment — it exists to catch what's easy to miss under time pressure, and to keep documentation clean enough that hospitals get paid for the treatment they've already given.

CuraMeds is built hospital-specific: each institution can configure its own clinical rules, ICD-10 setup, formulary, NHIA requirements, and branding, all on the same secure, offline-capable foundation.

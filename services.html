NHIA / Clinical Documentation & Coding

Hospitals can lose significant revenue when treatments aren't properly documented and coded for NHIA claims. CuraMeds uses AI, clinical information, and NHIA coding rules to help hospitals produce clear, clinically supported codes for every treatment.

The Problem

When codes are missing, incorrect, unclear, or poorly supported by the clinical documentation, claims may be delayed, reduced, rejected, or unpaid — even when the treatment was provided correctly.

The Solution

A clinician enters the patient's clinical information, and CuraMeds:

  • Understands the clinical information
  • Identifies the condition and treatment
  • Suggests the appropriate clinical/ICD-10 codes
  • Checks documentation against NHIA requirements
  • Flags missing information that could affect the claim
  • Produces a structured coding output for submission

The Goal

Help hospitals reduce rejected or poorly supported claims and improve the accuracy of NHIA reimbursement — ensuring every treatment is properly documented and coded. CuraMeds is designed as a hospital-wide solution, configurable to each hospital's clinical workflows, formularies, and NHIA requirements.

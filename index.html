About

Built to support clinical judgment, not replace it.

CuraMeds exists to make healthcare safer, smarter, and more accessible. We're starting with the moment it matters most: the point of care. A clinician enters a note or a medication list, and CuraMeds checks it against trusted guidelines — offline, encrypted, and fully explainable — so the final call always stays with the professional.

Trust

Patient data stays on-device. Built with a security-first architecture from day one, not bolted on later.

Precision

Every suggestion is grounded in WHO and NCDC guidance, with a citation attached — never an unexplained answer.

Calm

A point-of-care tool should reduce pressure, not add to it. Fast input, clear output, no clutter.

Features

What's in the first release

We're keeping the first version focused on two things, done well: differential diagnosis support and drug interaction checking — both offline, both explainable.

Differential diagnosis support

Enter a clinical note and get the 5 most relevant conditions to consider, drawn from trusted guideline sections.

Drug interaction analysis

Entered medications are checked against 17 major high-risk interactions — severity, explanation, and alert before treatment proceeds.

Offline clinical library

An encrypted, on-device knowledge base built on WHO and NCDC guidance — works with zero internet connection.

Transparent citations

Every suggestion links back to its source, so you can verify it independently — never a black-box answer.

About

Built to support clinical judgment, not replace it.

CuraMeds is a clinical intelligence platform for healthcare professionals and hospitals — combining AI, trusted guidelines, and hospital-specific configuration to support diagnosis, medication safety, coding, and documentation, end to end.

What we do

Clinical intelligence, end to end

From differential diagnosis at the bedside to ICD-10 coding and NHIA claims — one platform, fully explainable.

Differential diagnosis

Enter a clinical note and get the most relevant conditions to consider, drawn from trusted guideline sections.

Medication safety

Entered medications are checked for high-risk interactions — severity, explanation, and alert before treatment proceeds.

ICD-10 & NHIA coding

Clinical documentation mapped to the right codes, checked against NHIA requirements before claims go out.

Clinical audit trail

Every action is tracked, giving hospitals a foundation for accountability and auditing.
